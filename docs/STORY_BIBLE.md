## Dog Name Decision

They didn't adopt a pet as an afterthought — they **fed and raised** this dog together across the South Bay years. The dog is the emotional through-line: still there when the relationship isn't.

| Option | Why it fits | Why not (vs. winner) |
|--------|-------------|----------------------|
| **Scout** ✅ | Loyal, Texas-tough-adjacent, Bay Area trail energy; gender-neutral; easy to sing | — |
| Remy | Soft French nod to Zoë's blood | Slightly more "cute pet" than "raised together" grit |
| Pepper | Warm kitchen / feeding motif | Less cinematic for chorus hooks |

**Locked: Scout** — medium mutt energy, warm eyes, the animal who ate from both their hands.

## Character Looks (prompt-ready)

### Zoë Elise Hartmann
- **Age look:** mid-to-late 20s
- **Heritage mix (visual):** ~¼ German structure (strong jaw, high cheekbones) + ¼ French softness (fuller lips, refined nose) + Californian / light Mexican-Spanish warmth (olive-to-honey skin, dark brown eyes, sun-kissed undertone)
- **Hair:** dark brown, almost black in shade; long, slightly wavy; often half-up or loose for work; windblown in outdoor South Bay shots
- **Build / vibe:** confident posture, athletic-lean; Texas-tough swagger under California polish — chin up, direct gaze, not fragile
- **Wardrobe palette:** warm neutrals, black leather jacket over soft tees, tailored blazer for PM/startup, sundress for memory flashes; gold small hoop earrings
- **Roots cue:** San Jose / South Bay — 101 freeway light, Caltrain dusk, Berkeley campus green in flashbacks

### David Cole Ashford
- **Age look:** late 20s
- **Heritage / look:** California engineer; light-to-medium fair skin with easy Bay Area tan; brown hair (slightly wavy, casually styled); hazel or soft brown eyes
- **Build / vibe:** tall, clean lines, quiet confidence of someone raised with money but not flashy about it; hoodie-over-button-down engineer uniform
- **Wardrobe palette:** navy, grey, cream; premium casual (Uniqlo-meets-Allbirds energy); watch that reads understated wealth
- **Family cue:** multi-millionaire California family — taste shows in fit and fabrics, never logos

### Scout (the dog)
- **Breed vibe:** medium mixed-breed (shepherd-lab adjacent) — not purebred show dog
- **Coat:** warm caramel / light brown with cream chest; soft ears that fold halfway
- **Eyes:** bright amber-brown; always looking at Zoë or the empty space where David was
- **Size:** medium — fits on a couch between them, fits in a hatchback for weekend drives
- **Motif role:** feeding bowl, leash on the hook by the door, wet nose at the breakup silence

## Backstory Beats (locked)

1. **Roots:** San Jose / South Bay kid; Texas-tough confidence under California sun.
2. **School:** Berkeley grad.
3. **Career arc:** mobile ads **sales → PM** at a public company → **quits to found LumenReach** (startup grind + travel).
4. **Meet-cute:** work meetup; Zoë and David collide in the industry orbit.
5. **Relationship:** ~**5 years**; they raise **Scout** together — feedings, walks, the apartment that smelled like dog and takeout.
6. **Break:** startup travel and grind pull Zoë away; David can't live in the empty hours; breakup.
7. **Symbol:** Scout remains — the living archive of "we used to be us."
